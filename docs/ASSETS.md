# Asset provenance — Nordtor

All new visible assets were authored for this repository using Godot primitive meshes, transforms and materials. No downloaded asset packs, AI image services, paid content, external textures or unlicensed models are used.

- `Client/scripts/city.gd`: original building/road/gate/prop/skyline geometry, material batches and lighting.
- `Client/scripts/actor.gd`: original articulated humanoid, sword/shield, security drone, target ring and simple procedural walk/attack/death animations.
- `Client/scripts/joystick.gd` and `hud.gd`: original vector joystick and interface styling.
- `Client/icon.svg`: original repository application icon, retained from the foundation.
- Godot engine primitives, shaders and built-in font are supplied by Godot. Engine and bundled third-party notices remain governed by the Godot distribution; see https://godotengine.org/license/ and https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.3-stable/COPYRIGHT.txt.

These are economical stylized slice assets. Semi-realistic final art, sound and production animation remain pending. This provenance note does not introduce or change a repository-wide license.

M4 adds original procedural robes/staff/focus, bow/quiver, second blade, shield/helmet silhouettes, summon/pet variants, day/night light/fog and primitive particle rain in the same scripts. No third-party model, texture or audio is introduced. These remain stylized code-authored assets, not final production character rigs.

M5: `community_city.gd` authors modular cutaway apartment shells, doorways/windows, worktables/planters/chairs, quarter roads/lamps, arena markings, Tiefwerk columns and boss warning disks from original primitive meshes/materials. `actor.gd` adds original construct and AUR-IX silhouettes. No downloaded model/texture/audio, paid asset or unclear license was introduced. These are stylized original assets, not final semi-realistic production art or measured texture-compression/LOD assets.

Continuation: original procedural boar/forest-spirit/tree-boss meshes, additional forest/bridge/ruin scenery, avatar face/body/hair/skin/tattoo parameters and equipped weapon/chest overlays. Original minimap and graphics-profile UI. No third-party model/texture/audio licenses introduced; final semi-realistic rigs, textures and extensive animations remain unimplemented.
